# UX Writing

## Overview
UX writing helps users understand information, complete tasks, and recover from errors. Clear and consistent content is especially important for Greenstand's global audience, which includes users with varying literacy levels, languages, and digital experience.


## Guidelines
### Use plain language
Use simple, familiar words and short sentences. Avoid technical terms, jargon, and unnecessary complexity.


### Use active voice
Write direct and action oriented content.

- ✅ Upload photos to continue
- ❌ Photos should be uploaded to continue

### Be specific
Clearly describe actions, outcomes, and status.

- ✅ 12 photos waiting to upload
- ❌ Pending items

### Support text with visuals
Use icons and other visual cues to reinforce meaning, especially for actions, status information, and important messages.

### Design for localization
Write content that can be easily translated into supported languages, including English, Swahili, and Portuguese. 

- ❌ Avoid idioms, slang, and culturally specific references.

### Error messages
Error messages should help users understand the situation and recover quickly. Explain what happened, why it happened (if known), and what to do next.

- ✅ Upload failed because there is no internet connection. Connect to the internet and try again.
- ❌ Upload failed.
