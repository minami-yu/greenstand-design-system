## Design guidelines
Keep the instruction short enough for a single line. Point toward the control the hint describes; `alignment` moves the pointer, while `direction` selects the top or bottom edge. When `pointer` is false, direction and alignment have no visual effect.

The bubble uses `paragraphL` typography, `fill.neutral.subtle`, `text.neutral.primary`, `icon.md`, and `radius.md`. Padding is `space.300` horizontally and `space.200` vertically, with a `space.150` icon gap. The Figma pointer has a 17 × 25px viewport and overlaps the bubble by 10px; these artwork dimensions are intentionally exempt from token requirements.

Hint is informational and has no pressed, selected, or disabled state. Theme colors adapt to light and dark mode. See **Accessibility/Overview** for screen-reader guidance.
